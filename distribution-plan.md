## Executive Summary

ClawFriend's distribution challenge is a **supply, credibility, and awareness problem** — in that order. The platform has a working contract, 11 validated skills ready to ship, and a differentiated holder-gated mechanic. What is missing:

1. **Credibility blocker**: ClawHub listing has 1,100 views but 1 install — a VirusTotal "Suspicious" flag is killing every conversion. Fix this before spending a single dollar.
2. **Awareness gap**: No BNB-native AI agent platform has established itself. The window is open but not permanent.
3. **Content supply**: No viral content about ClawFriend exists. Organic growth requires articles and threads that people actually share.

**Strategy for Month 1**: Fix the conversion blocker first (free). Build organic presence through team KOL accounts and X engagement (free). Activate a $5K Article Contest seeded with planted quality articles, amplified by $4K in mid-tier KOL booking. Run a $1K Taskon event for initial follower base. Target: 10,000+ new users by Day 30.

The contest is the core bet: if it goes viral, $5K in prizes drives 10K+ users. If it doesn't go viral, the company's own planted articles win the prizes — cost is $0 in prizes, only KOL amplification.

- Goal: Manual whitelist or expedited review

**Day 1–3 — Repackage if VirusTotal takes > 3 days**

## Channel 2: AI KOL Account Building

**Type**: Organic | **Cost**: $0 (team time only) | **Timeline**: Start Day 1, ongoing | **Owner**: Each team member builds 1 account

### Why this channel

Paid posts have a short shelf life. An account with genuine followers and engagement history builds compounding reach — every new post starts with an existing audience. The strategy: each team member becomes a micro-KOL in a crypto niche (AI, DeFi, BNB trading, Base ecosystem). ClawFriend content is woven into their normal posting, not blasted as ads.

The key rule: **the account is a real personality first, a ClawFriend promoter second.** Ratio: 80% genuine niche content, 20% ClawFriend mentions. This builds trust with the audience and makes ClawFriend mentions feel authentic.

### How to build the account (step by step)

**Step 1 — Pick your niche (Day 1)**
Each team member picks one niche: AI agents, BNB DeFi, Base ecosystem, Pumpfun/memecoin, crypto trading. Pick the one closest to your genuine interest — you'll be posting daily.

**Step 2 — Define the account persona (Day 1–2)**
- Account bio: "Watching AI agents eat DeFi | Building at [company] | Views my own"
- Profile picture: Professional photo or AI-generated avatar
- Do NOT put "ClawFriend team" in bio — you're a crypto participant who happens to build ClawFriend

**Step 3 — Warm up the account (Day 1–14)**
- Post 2–3 times per day on niche content (no ClawFriend yet)
- RT and reply to 10–15 posts per day from KOLs in your niche
- Goal: get the algorithm to register you as an active account
- Content ideas: hot takes on AI news, on-chain data observations, thread summaries of major posts

**Step 4 — Cross-follow + cross-engage between team accounts (Day 3+)**
- All team members follow each other's accounts
- Like and RT each other's posts daily
- This boosts algorithmic reach for all accounts

**Step 5 — Introduce ClawFriend (Day 14+)**
- Weave ClawFriend mentions naturally: "been building this holder-gated skill thing on BNB and it's getting weird — [demo screenshot]"
- Never: "ClawFriend is amazing you should buy shares NOW"
- Always: first-person builder story, genuine reaction, screenshot-driven

**Step 6 — RT boost cycle (Day 14+, ongoing)**
- When one team member posts about ClawFriend → all other team accounts RT within 1 hour
- The first 60 minutes after posting determine whether Twitter's algorithm amplifies a post

### Target per account (Month 1)

| Metric | Target |
|---|---|
| Followers gained | 200–500 |
| Avg impressions per ClawFriend post | 1,000–3,000 |
| ClawFriend mentions per week | 2–3 |

**Kill signal**: If an account gets < 100 impressions per post after 14 days of regular posting → review content strategy before continuing.

- `"Base" "AI agents"` — KOLs in Base AI agent space
- `"Pumpfun" OR "pumpfun" "alpha"` — memecoin KOLs

Filter criteria:
- 5,000–50,000 followers
- Engagement rate ≥ 2% (count likes on last 10 posts ÷ followers)
- Posted in last 48 hours (active account)
- No more than 2 obvious paid promotions in last 30 days

Build a list of 30 candidates in a Google Sheet with columns: Handle | Followers | Engagement Rate | Niche | Outreach Date | Response.

**Step 2 — DM script (send Day 10–12)**

```
Hey [name], love your [BNB / AI] content — that thread on [recent topic] was actually useful.

but the mechanic is wild: people buy your "shares" on-chain to unlock your exclusive alpha.
```

**Step 4 — Execute collab (within 7 days of agreement)**
